One Copilot Studio agent for the people you hope will join you: future students, and future staff and faculty. Choose who is asking, then open the journey.

Same agent, two audiences. The public-facing side is published to your website with No authentication, so it can only use public information (program pages, careers pages). The internal side, for admissions counselors, recruiters, hiring managers and search committees, is published to Microsoft Teams with Authenticate with Microsoft (Entra ID). In practice many institutions build these as two agents in one environment that share child agents and flows.
